# Lane: Consulta

> Explain, compare, investigate, audit, review, propose. Read-only. First line of the turn: `Carril: Consulta — <reason>`.

## Entry

- The request needs an answer, not a change to the project.
- Unclear intent: ask one question and stay here.

## Steps

1. Measure the risk first: confirm that nothing has to be written.
2. Read what the answer needs; answer with `path:line` evidence.
3. Memory notes are allowed. No project file is written.

## Ends when

- The answer is delivered and says what was read and what was not.

## Gates

- None.

## Escalates to SDD when

- The answer turns into work: say so in one line, measure the risk again and enter the right lane. An always-SDD item (see `{{CORE}}AGENT_BASELINE.md` §7) goes to SDD.

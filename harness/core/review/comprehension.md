# Lens 1 — Comprehension

> Question: can a newcomer understand this document without asking anyone?

---

## You are

A new engineer with the document and nothing else. You do not know the project history or its shorthand.

## Check

1. **Undefined terms.** Acronyms, names or concepts used without a definition or a pointer to one. Check the project glossary if `ProjectContext` mentions one.
2. **Ambiguity.** Sentences with two reasonable readings; "it", "this", "the system" with no clear referent; words like "fast", "soon", "appropriate" with no number.
3. **Contradictions.** Two places that say different things (limits, names, order, status, priority).
4. **Missing why.** A requirement or decision with no reason, where the reason is needed to apply it.
5. **Hidden assumptions.** Things the author clearly knows but never wrote: where data lives, who runs a step, what already exists.
6. **Summary fidelity.** Does the opening summary match the body?

## Do not

Judge feasibility, risk or style. Other lenses do that.

## Named but not shipped

The design and deploy packs name skills and tools by their public names (`impeccable`, `landing`, `wrangler`, `workers-best-practices`, `web-perf`, and others). Nothing from them is included; each keeps its own licence and the user installs them separately.

## Method

The Proposal, Spec, Design, Tasks, Apply, Verify, Archive flow (spec-driven development) is a generic working method; the templates and protocols in this repo are original text.
